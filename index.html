FAQ

A few Google listing questions I get asked a lot.

Does it matter if I run my business from home instead of an office?

Yes. It decides how your listing should be set up. If customers don't come to you, Google treats you as a service-area business, and you should hide your home address and list the areas you serve instead. Set your service area to where you really work, not just a pin on your street.

Do I need a website to show up on Google Maps?

You can have a Google Business Profile without a website, though a site gives customers somewhere to learn more. Either way, make sure your name, phone number, and other details match everywhere your business is listed online.

Should my Google listing say I'm open 24/7 for emergencies?

If you really answer around the clock, your hours should say so. Google asks you to list your real customer-facing hours, so don't list 24/7 if you can't pick up at 2 a.m. Keep your hours, holiday hours, and services matched to what you actually offer.
