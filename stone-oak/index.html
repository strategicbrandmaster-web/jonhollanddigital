Why Stone Oak is its own market

Stone Oak is a master-planned community. Its developers filed the plan back in the 1980s, and the property owners association still keeps sign and height rules. Under the POA, the area is split into dozens of neighborhoods: Sonterra, Arrowhead, The Villages of Stone Oak, The Hills of Stone Oak, Champions Ridge, Big Springs, and many more, each with its own HOA.

That matters for marketing. When neighbors swap recommendations within an HOA, the name they pass along is the one they find on Google with good photos and recent reviews. And if you're mailing postcards, you can target Sonterra or the neighborhoods off TPC Parkway and Evans Road on their own, instead of blanketing all of north San Antonio.

One more local quirk: lots of people call everything north of 1604 between Blanco and 281 "Stone Oak," even though the official boundaries are tighter. Your Google listing should describe your real service area the way your customers describe it.
