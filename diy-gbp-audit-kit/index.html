How it works

Check it, score it, fix it.

  1. Check

    Go through the checklist workbook and mark each point Pass, Fix, or Unverified. Plan on about an hour, in one or two sittings.

  2. Score

    The workbook adds up your score and lists your top 3 fixes.

  3. Fix

    Follow the 30-day plan and work through your fixes a little at a time.

This kit helps you find the easy wins on your listing. It doesn't promise rankings, calls, or sales.

FAQ

Questions about the kit.

How long does it take?

Plan on about an hour for the full 33-point check, in one or two sittings. You can also do a section at a time.

Do I need to buy any tools?

No. Every check uses free tools, like Google Maps, PageSpeed Insights, and Google's Rich Results Test.

I'm not techy. Is this for me?

Yes. It's written in plain English, with short steps and links to Google's own help pages.

Will this get me to the top of Google Maps?

I can't promise that, and no one honestly can. The kit helps you find what's helping and what's holding your profile back, so you can fix the easy things first.

Is this connected to Google?

No. It's an independent guide, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
