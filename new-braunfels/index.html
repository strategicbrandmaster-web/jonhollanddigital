Google Business Profile help for New Braunfels businesses

New Braunfels is a town of two rivers, the Comal and the Guadalupe, sitting on I-35 between San Antonio and Austin. It has a historic downtown around Main Plaza and Seguin Avenue, Gruene to the north, and newer growth around Creekside Town Center. Busy stretches like Walnut Avenue and Loop 337 tie it all together. And between Comal and Guadalupe counties, there's no shortage of work for local service businesses.

I'm Jon Holland, a one-person marketing shop based in San Antonio. I work with New Braunfels businesses remotely and by appointment. I don't have an office in New Braunfels and won't pretend to.

A market with seasons

Summer river season changes the rhythm here. The city runs its river parking season from May through Labor Day, and plenty of businesses feel it: cleaners and property managers turning over short-term rentals, pool and AC techs, pest control, landscapers, auto shops, and food and retail businesses near the rivers. Then the rest of the year, it's steady home services for the neighborhoods spreading out from town.

That makes your Google listing worth a look a couple of times a year. Holiday and seasonal hours, current photos, and posts about what you're doing right now all help a customer decide quickly.

Gruene, downtown, Creekside, and everything in between

People search the way they talk. Some say "Gruene," some say "New Braunfels," and some just search "near me." Your service area and website wording should cover how your customers actually describe where they are, without stuffing in every place name.

For print, New Braunfels works well for targeted mailers: one neighborhood or one side of I-35 at a time, so you can see what pulls calls before spending more.
