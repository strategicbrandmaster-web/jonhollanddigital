Privacy

Last updated October 2026

What I collect

When you request a free audit, the form asks for:

  • Your business name
  • The area you serve (optional)
  • The email address for the report
  • A link to your Google listing (optional)
  • Anything else you choose to type in the notes box (optional)

If you email or call me, I'll also have whatever you include in that email or call.

Why I collect it

I use it only to prepare and send your audit and to follow up with you about it. I don't sell it, share it for anyone else's marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

Who handles it

  • Formspree receives the form and forwards it to me by email. Formspree has its own privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may log basic technical details of each visit, such as IP address and browser type.

The fonts on this site are served from this site itself, and the site doesn't run advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Deleting your information

To ask me to delete what you've sent, email jon@jonhollanddigital.com and I'll take care of it.
