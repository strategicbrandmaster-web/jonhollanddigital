Boerne has a small-town feel and a lot of new rooftops. There's the historic downtown along Main Street, the stretch known as the Hill Country Mile. Newer communities like Esperanza, Menger Springs, and the Ranches at Creekside keep growing, and Cordillera Ranch and Tapatio Springs sit out in the hills nearby. Getting found along I-10 and beyond

Boerne sits on I-10 northwest of San Antonio, and State Highway 46 cuts across town. That means your customers might be searching from Boerne itself, from the neighborhoods off I-10 and SH 46, or from out in Kendall County. If your service area on Google is vague or out of date, you can disappear for the exact searches you want.

Here's what I check first for a Boerne business:

  • Is your service area set to where you really work, not just a pin on your street?
  • Do your categories and services match what people actually search for?
  • Do your photos show real jobs in Hill Country homes, not stock images?
  • Is your name, address, and phone the same everywhere online?

Print that fits the town

Boerne's historic district has its own rules for signs, so for downtown shops, print often does more of the talking. Postcards, door hangers, and good business cards still work well here, especially for reaching a single neighborhood at a time.
